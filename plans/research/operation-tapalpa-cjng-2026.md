## Real-world facts (anchor)

### CJNG (Cártel Jalisco Nueva Generación)
- Founded ~2009 from remnants of Milenio Cartel
- Headquartered in Jalisco state, Mexico
- One of the most powerful and violent cartels globally as of mid-2020s
- Activities: methamphetamine, fentanyl, cocaine trafficking; extortion; territory control
- Known for paramilitary tactics, IEDs, drone-dropped explosives, .50-cal mounted trucks

### Leader: Nemesio Oseguera Cervantes "El Mencho"
- Real name: Rubén Oseguera (alias El Mencho)
- DOB: 1966, Michoacán
- Background: former Mexican federal police, deported from US in 1990s
- **FBI/DEA bounty: $15 MILLION USD** (highest ever for a Mexican drug trafficker)
- Status: At-large as of late 2025; multiple near-capture incidents
- Profile: thick mustache, broad shoulders, often photographed only from behind / in shadow

### Geography: Tapalpa
- Town in southern Jalisco, ~120km south of Guadalajara
- Sierra Madre Occidental foothills, elevation ~2080m (~6,800 ft)
- Pine and oak forest, fog-shrouded valleys, ranch land
- Long history as cartel safe-haven territory due to terrain + remoteness
- Notable locations nearby: Lago de Atotonilco, Las Piedrotas, Sierra de Tapalpa

### Mexican Marines (SEMAR — Secretaría de Marina)
- Lead anti-cartel high-value target operations
- Standard kit:
  - **FX-05 Xiuhcoatl** (5.56mm assault rifle, Mexican-made)
  - Multicam Tropic fatigues
  - Ops-Core helmets with NVG mounts
  - Body armor with integrated radio
- Air assets: **UH-60 Black Hawk** (Mexican Marine green livery, white SEMAR insignia)
- Famous for operations: capture of El Chapo (2014), Ovidio Guzmán (2019, 2023)
